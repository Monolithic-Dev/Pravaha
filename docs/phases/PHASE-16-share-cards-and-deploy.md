## Objective
Make every shared link look like a product, and put Pravaha live.
- **Share cards:** Open Graph images generated by Cloudinary for each session and Moment: the `g_auto` frame at that moment, a title/speaker text overlay and the Pravaha mark, as one image URL. WhatsApp, LinkedIn and Slack previews look designed.
- **Moment landing page `/m/[segmentId]`:** a shared Moment opens a branded page (vertical clip, the quote, "Watch the full session", "Ask the library") instead of a raw mp4. This is the growth loop in `VISION.md`.
- **Deploy:** Vercel project, production env vars, `APP_URL`, and the preset's `notification_url` pointed at the live webhook (`pnpm spike` sets it); then `pnpm test:e2e` against production

## As Built
- `shareCardUrl()` (`src/lib/media.ts`, unit-tested): 1200×630 `g_auto` frame at the moment + `e_gradient_fade:symmetric_pad` (top and bottom darkened for text, the speaker in the middle stays bright) + Pravaha mark + title + subtitle, `f_jpg`. Verified on real Cloudinary output; titles over 90 characters are truncated.
- `/m/[segmentId]`: the vertical Moment (`MomentClip`, shared with the dialog, waits through the `423` tracking-crop), the quote, title/speaker/chapter, **Watch the full session**, **Share this Moment**, and an **Ask the library** bar. Open Graph `video.other` with the share card as `og:image` and the Moment `.mp4` as `og:video`; Twitter `summary_large_image`. A page view counts as a Moment `open` in Insights.
- Every share (dialog and page) sends the `/m/<id>` page URL, not the raw `.mp4`.
- `/watch/[id]` gets the same card (title · speaker · duration).
- Unlisted sessions: Moment and Watch pages stay reachable by link but are `noindex`.
- `metadataBase` comes from `APP_URL` (root layout).
- `scripts/env.mjs` falls back to `.env`, so `pnpm spike` can set the preset's `notification_url` from any setup.

## Acceptance Criteria
Pasting a Moment link into WhatsApp shows a designed preview; the live site ingests an upload through the real webhook with no local tooling.
